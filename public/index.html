Questions

Questions companies ask us first

Short answers. For anything else, the contact form below reaches a person, not a queue.

How is training delivered?
On site at your premises, live online, or self-paced on the platform. Most in-house programmes combine two: a live cohort with self-paced modules before and after.
Arabic or English?
Both. Each learner picks their own language on the portal, and materials, quizzes and certificates follow. Trainers deliver in whichever the cohort needs.
Do participants get a certificate?
Yes, on every course marked as certified. Each certificate carries a code that anyone can check on this site, so a recruiter or auditor can verify it without contacting us.
Can we run our own content on the platform?
Yes. Your company gets its own branded portal where you can add videos, PDFs and quizzes next to our programmes, assign them by department, and read completion reports in one place.
How quickly can a programme start?
Scoping takes about a week — a short call, then a written outline. Delivery follows in weeks, not months. Open-cohort dates for individuals are available on request.
How is pricing structured?
Per cohort for in-house delivery, per seat for open courses, and a licence for the company platform. Every proposal is itemised, and you have it within two working days of your enquiry.
