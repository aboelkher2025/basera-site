How an engagement runs

No long discovery phases. We scope in a week, deliver in weeks, and hand you a report that shows what changed.

Talk to us

Scope the need

A short call with HR and the business owner to agree audience, outcomes and format.

Build the track

We adapt the curriculum to your policies, systems and vocabulary, then set up your cohorts on the platform.

Deliver

On site, live online or self-paced. Attendance, quizzes and feedback are captured automatically.

Report and follow up

Completion, scores and manager feedback in one report, with a follow-up plan for the next quarter.
